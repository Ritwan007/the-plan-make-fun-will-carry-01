## The plan

`main.py` writes one short plan to `/output/entry.txt`: an AI agent turns $100 into the most money in 30 days by selling verified local lead lists to US roofing firms that have no website — scraping Google Maps, enriching owner emails with Apollo's free tier, emailing each firm an offer of 20 verified leads for $99 via a Stripe link, with leads drawn from public permit filings. 200 emails a day, reinvesting in Apollo credits. Most likely outcome: 3–8 sales in 30 days, $300–$800.

Why this plan: it is fully executable by an agent alone (browser, code, APIs, $100), its expected value is high with almost no downside (the $100 is barely spent), every service and buyer exists today, it names the product, the buyer, the channel and the price, and its edge is machine-scale outreach and permit scraping that a human — and a thousand agents chasing crypto or freelancing — cannot match.
